Raider Labs Lesson Kit

Every piece of the lesson model, working on one page, for whoever builds the next lesson. Two subjects are mixed on purpose (chemistry and physics) to show the pieces aren't tied to one lab. How to use it: kit/README.md.

1. Pick a way in · LK.path

A mystery the lesson solves, or just the lesson the way the teacher teaches it. The choice is saved.

2. Show change as motion · LK.stepper + LK.bars

Each step changes the picture and highlights what changed. Play runs it; Next step goes one at a time (and is all that shows with reduced motion). The weight bars appear on the last step.

3. Road map strip on a real problem · LK.strip + number-only boxes + LK.confused

The problem's own numbers fill in as each step is solved. Answer boxes take numbers only. "This confused me" helps first, then asks why.
